    About Claimlight

    A map, not medical care.

    Claimlight turns a health statement into questions about evidence: what was studied, in whom, compared with what, and what was not tested. It uses AI and web search to assemble each dossier.

    It does not diagnose, determine whether something is safe or effective for you, or replace guidance from a qualified professional. Research coverage can vary, so the app shows its sources and uncertainty instead of a certainty badge.
